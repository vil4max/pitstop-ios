PitStop on iOS 27

Home is the car. Everything else is a view into its memory. The redesign keeps that root idea and the delivered behaviour, and changes how the surfaces are built: three tiers instead of a card for everything.

Tier 1 · Stage

  • Car Hero and the Road lane are the only tinted, custom-drawn surfaces. They carry the identity: the car, the road, Pit's eyes.
  • One calm accent (cloud blue) tints the stage at 10–22 %; nothing else is tinted.

Tier 2 · Grouped lists

  • Service, History, Notes and the Road milestone list become one inset-grouped container per section, not one 26 pt card per row. Rows read as a list; sections read as sections.
  • Summary tiles on Car Board stay cards: they are entrances, and a card is the affordance.

Tier 3 · Glass controls

  • Liquid Glass only for what floats: the utility layer, toolbar actions, "Back to now", the sheet's primary action.
  • State is never colour alone: a status chip is a word plus a glyph plus a colour. Red stays reserved for danger.

pit-behavior-and-motion.md · product-design.md "Pit visual hypothesis" · owner request 2026-09-22

Pit, the character

The owner asked for a full image of Pit with a head, with EVE (WALL-E's friend) as the reference. Pit becomes a small companion who peeks from behind the interface: an original round head with a dark face screen, and the chosen lens eyes lit on that screen. From the reference it borrows mechanics only: lit eyes on a dark glass face, expression by eye shape, a slight tilt of the head. EVE's upright egg shape, arms, glow colour and proportions are not copied. Pit still has no body, no mouth and no hands: the face screen and the eyes do all the acting.

Pit at rest — chosen by the owner (2026-09-22). Pearl-white round head, navy visor, lit lens eyes with a halo.

Anatomy

  • Head: a sphere, pearl white with a soft top-left light and a hairline. In the utility layer the whole 56 pt circle is Pit's head: there is no glass disc behind it.
  • Face screen: deep navy (the icon's dark fill), glossy highlight, faint blue glow behind the eyes.
  • Eyes: the approved lens pair, lit in soft blue-white with a small highlight and a halo, no pupils; on a knock they turn cloud blue and the glow strengthens (REQ-PIT-023).
  • Face screen bezel: a thin grey rim sets the screen into the shell, like a visor.

Motion

  • The eyes carry every state of the motion table (ADR 0028), unchanged.
  • The head adds only two moves: a tilt of up to 6° (thinking rolls aside, knock leans in) and a lift of up to 3 pt (startle, knock). No bobbing or idle sway: the head is still unless a state asks.
  • Reduce Motion: poses change without animation, knock is the lifted, lit pose.
