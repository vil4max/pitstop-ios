PitStop on iOS 27

Home is the car. Everything else is a view into its memory. The redesign keeps that root idea and the delivered behaviour, and changes how the surfaces are built: three tiers instead of a card for everything.

Tier 1 · Stage

  • Car Hero and the Road lane are the only tinted, custom-drawn surfaces. They carry the identity: the car, the road, Pit's eyes.
  • One calm accent (cloud blue) tints the stage at 10–22 %; nothing else is tinted.

Tier 2 · Grouped lists

  • Service, History, Notes and the Road milestone list become one inset-grouped container per section, not one 26 pt card per row. Rows read as a list; sections read as sections.
  • Summary tiles on Car Board stay cards: they are entrances, and a card is the affordance.

Tier 3 · Glass controls

  • Liquid Glass only for what floats: the utility layer, toolbar actions, "Back to now", the sheet's primary action.
  • State is never colour alone: a status chip is a word plus a glyph plus a colour. Red stays reserved for danger.

Eye reference analysis owner reference 2026-09-22

The owner pointed at two Pixar robots as a reference for Pit's gaze: EVE (two lit shapes on a dark screen, expression by shape) and WALL-E (two separate binocular eyes, expression by tilt and by looking around). What follows borrows mechanics only. No head, visor, stalks, glow, colour or proportion of either character is copied; Pit stays two shapes with no body (product-design.md "Pit visual hypothesis").

Recommendation: EVE's shape language with WALL-E's independence. Each eye is a lit lens whose outline carries the state; the two lenses tilt and move independently by a few degrees (the delivered 25 ms trailing-eye offset already makes them two pieces, ADR 0028). Inward-top tilt is capped at 6° and used only for attention (knock), never for judgement. The closed state becomes a shallow upward arc, so "completion / leaving" reads as content rather than switched off. Nothing else in the motion table, the idle policy or the timings changes.

Profile scope stays photo, name and mileage; no make, model or specifications on the hero (REQ-BOARD-017).

The reference image itself is not embedded yet: it arrived only in chat, and it ships only once its source and a permissive licence are recorded, or it is redrawn as original artwork in the same style.

Behaviour

  • Subject lift runs on device (Vision foreground instance mask); the original and the cut-out are stored in the app container, referenced from the car context; the photo never enters analytics or a widget without the App Group.

Layer geometry. 56 pt circles, 20 pt from the edges, above the home indicator; scroll content is inset by the layer height so the last row is never covered (REQ-UTILITY-007, 008).

Rules

  • Glass is interactive regular glass in a circle; no label under the circles; VoiceOver labels "Settings" and "Pit", with "Has a question" as Pit's value on a knock.
  • Keyboard: text input lives in sheets that cover the layer; the layer never rides up (ADR 0009, resolves the contract's INVESTIGATE).
  • Sheets and full-screen covers cover the layer; on return it is where it was.

Dynamic Type

  • Circles and glyphs keep their size at every text size; the two never overlap because they are pinned to opposite edges (REQ-UTILITY-010).
