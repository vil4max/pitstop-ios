## Context

The iOS 27 redesign gives Pit a round head: a pearl shell, a navy face screen
set in a thin bezel and the two lit lens eyes (owner decision 2026-09-22). The
proposal first kept the icon eyes-only (REQ-ICON-001). The owner then decided
that the icon shows the new Pit and ships at once, on `main`, before any
redesign card: the icon is the first visible piece of the redesign and
announces it, and RD-000 is the next step.

## Decision

- **Motif:** Pit's round head at rest, nothing else. REQ-ICON-001 now reads
  "the head with the face screen and the two eyes at rest".
- **Geometry source:** the resting pose of `pitHead()` in
  [`../design/ios27-mockups.html`](../design/ios27-mockups.html) (56-unit
  view box): head circle r 27 at (28, 28); bezel 40 x 25.5 at (8, 16.5),
  radius 12.75; visor 38 x 23.5 at (9, 17.5), radius 11.75; lens eyes
  rx 4.1, ry 5.9 at (22, 29.2) and (34, 29.2), tilted 6° outward; highlight
  rx 1.3, ry 1.7 at (-1.1, -2.3) in each eye's frame. Scaled uniformly by
  15.2 px per unit and centred on the 1024 canvas, the head spans
  x 102–922 (80 % of the width). The in-app head does not exist yet;
  RD-011 draws it from the same geometry, so the icon and the utility
  control stay one face (REQ-ICON-002).
- **Layers** (flat fills only, per ADR 0029 and REQ-ICON-006):
  `1-head` #F3F5F8, `2-bezel` #C9CFD8, `3-visor` #14223A, `4-eyes` #D9E9FF,
  `5-highlights` #FFFFFF. The mockup's shell gradient, gloss arc, hairline,
  eye halo and visor glow are not drawn: they would be baked effects. The
  shell's light and the eyes' shine come from Icon Composer's glass and
  specular.
- **Groups (back to front):** `1-head` glass, specular, neutral shadow 0.5,
  translucency 0.15; `2-face` (bezel and visor) flat, no specular or shadow,
  so the screen stays a solid dark field; `3-eyes` glass with specular, no
  shadow or translucency, so the eyes stay lit on the visor; `4-highlights`
  flat. Four groups is Icon Composer's maximum.
- **Fills and appearances:** unchanged from ADR 0029 (cloud-blue gradient,
  navy dark specialization, system clear and tinted).
- **Generator:** the layers are generated by a script kept with the task's
  agent artifacts (`agent-artifacts/2026-09-22/icon-002-pit-head/`), not in
  the repository; the numbers above are enough to redraw them.

## Verified

- `ictool` renders of all six appearances (design generation 27) and 60 and
  40 px downscales on light and dark grounds: the head reads against every
  background, and the two eyes stay separate on the visor. Tinted dark is the
  lowest-contrast appearance: the head takes the tint and the visor stays
  near black, so the eyes still separate.
- `just verify` with the default Xcode: see the commit body.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Keep the icon eyes-only until RD-011.** The owner wants the icon to show
  the new Pit now, as the announcement of the redesign.
- **Bake the mockup's shading into the SVGs.** Against Apple's Icon Composer
  guidance and REQ-ICON-006; Liquid Glass supplies the light.
- **Head without the bezel.** The visor then touches the pearl shell directly
  and loses the set-in face screen that makes the head read as Pit, not a
  generic mask.

## Consequences

- Until RD-011 lands, the app shows Pit's eyes in the glass circle while the
  icon shows the head. This mismatch is accepted by the owner.
- RD-011 must draw the in-app head from this geometry. If RD-011 changes the
  geometry, it regenerates the icon in the same card.

## Open

- Dark, clear and tinted Home Screen check on a device (as ADR 0029).
