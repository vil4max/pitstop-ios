# Release 1.2.0

TestFlight 1.2.0 ships the iOS 27 redesign round (RD-000…RD-011, FU-1…FU-5)
and the RD-012 car profile pilot round. SYS-008 follows in 1.3.0 (owner,
2026-09-24).

## What to Test (draft)

This list is the device checklist (kit KIT-D-027): the `tf-1.2.0-…`
annotation is written from it. RD-012 closed with no Deferred row (every
cited requirement passed in the matrix), so its device-only checks are the
items below. Each item is one pass/fail check on the build
named in its line. It replaces the work-plan rows DEV-WIDGET and
DEV-PIT-SHEET.

Build for every item: the first 1.2.0 TestFlight build (`tf-1.2.0-1`), until
a result below names another.

### From DEV-WIDGET (ADR 0025, ADR 0026, ADR 0036)

1. **Upgrade keeps everything** — REQ-WIDGET-001, REQ-WIDGET-002.
   Steps: with the 1.1.0 build installed and some cars, notes, events and
   tracked operations recorded, update to 1.2.0 from TestFlight and open the
   app. PASS if every car, note, event and tracked operation is still there.
2. **Widget gallery** — REQ: none (ADR 0025). Steps: long-press the Home
   Screen, add a widget, search Pitstop. PASS if both "Remember" and "Next
   service" are offered, in their Home Screen and Lock Screen sizes.
3. **"Remember" widget, tinted and dark** — REQ: none (ADR 0025, RD-009).
   Steps: place it on the Home Screen, then switch the Home Screen to tinted
   and to dark icons. PASS if the whole glyph disc takes the tint, and the
   disc and its text stay legible in dark.
4. **"Next service" widget, tinted, dark and Lock Screen** — REQ-WIDGET-004,
   REQ-WIDGET-011, REQ-WIDGET-012. Steps: place it on the Home Screen with a
   tracked operation, switch to tinted and dark; add the rectangular widget
   to the Lock Screen; turn on VoiceOver and touch it. PASS if the eyebrow
   takes the tint, the chip and its glyph stay legible in the default and
   dark appearances, the status glyph reads by its shape in the Lock Screen
   tint, and VoiceOver reads the whole content.
5. **"Open Pit" control and Lock Screen widgets** — REQ: none (ADR 0025).
   Steps: add the "Open Pit" control to Control Center and to the Lock
   Screen, and the rectangular and inline widgets to the Lock Screen; tap
   each. PASS if each is offered in its gallery, the control opens PitStop
   with the Pit sheet, and each widget opens the app.
6. **Action button and Shortcuts** — REQ: none (ADR 0026 checks 16 and 18).
   Steps: assign the "Open Pit" control to the Action button and press it
   with the app terminated; open Shortcuts → App Shortcuts → PitStop and
   search "Open Pit" in the action list. PASS if PitStop opens with the Pit
   sheet, and Remember and Open Pit are listed, Open Pit once.
7. **App Group in the Xcode Cloud archive** — REQ-WIDGET-009 (ADR 0036).
   Steps: on the installed TestFlight build, record a completion and look at
   the "Next service" widget. PASS if the widget shows the new state without
   opening the app again.

### From DEV-PIT-SHEET (REQ-UTILITY-012, REQ-PIT-026)

8. **Pit in every sheet** — REQ-UTILITY-012. Steps: open each sheet — car
   editor, note, history event, planned date, Track an operation, Track
   several, Mark as done, dashboard reading — and Settings, at the medium
   and at the large detent. PASS if Pit sits at the bottom-trailing spot in
   each, and Settings is not shown over the sheet.
9. **Pit above the keyboard** — REQ-UTILITY-012. Steps: in the note sheet
   and the car editor, bring up the keyboard, at the default and the largest
   accessibility text size, in portrait and landscape. PASS if Pit rides
   above the keyboard and covers neither a trailing control of the sheet nor
   a keyboard accessory.
10. **Pit disabled while a sheet saves** — REQ-PIT-026. Steps: save in Track
    several, the planned date editor and the dashboard reading sheet, and
    watch Pit during the save. PASS if Pit is dimmed and does not respond
    until the save finishes.
11. **Capture over a sheet** — REQ-PIT-026. Steps: type something into a
    sheet, tap Pit in it, remember a thought, close capture. PASS if capture
    opens over the sheet, and on closing the sheet is back with what was
    typed.

### From RD-012 (car profile; ADR 0040)

12. **The car without a photo** — REQ-DESIGN-005, REQ-BOARD-030,
    REQ-BOARD-032. Steps: on a car with no photo, look at Car Board, the Road
    tile and Road; in the car editor choose Sedan and save, then SUV. PASS if
    no step asked for a photo, the car is a grey side-view SUV (then sedan,
    then SUV again) facing right on all three, and nothing names a make or
    model.
13. **Choose a car photo** — REQ-BOARD-029, REQ-BOARD-031. Steps: in the car
    editor tap "Choose photo", pick a photo of a car from your library, save.
    PASS if no permission prompt appeared, the stage shows your car cut out
    onto it or, if it could not be cut out, the whole photo in the rounded
    frame, with no error; Road and the Road tile show the same car.
14. **Remove the photo** — REQ-BOARD-033. Steps: tap "Remove photo", save.
    PASS if the car returns to the placeholder for the chosen body.
15. **The car avatar** — REQ-BOARD-034. Steps: open Road, Notes, History and
    Service; remember something in Pit until "Saved."; answer one of Pit's
    questions; repeat at the largest accessibility text size. PASS if a
    small round picture of your car sits before the car's name on each
    screen's header, beside "Saved." and on the question card, the words
    wrap under it at the largest size, and Car Board's own header and
    Settings show none.
16. **The car editor** — REQ-BOARD-033. Steps: pick a photo, tap Save and
    at once try Cancel or swiping the sheet down; separately pick a photo
    that cannot be loaded (for example an iCloud-only original with the
    network off). PASS if the sheet cannot be closed until the save ends,
    and a failed pick says "This photo could not be loaded" and saves
    nothing new.
